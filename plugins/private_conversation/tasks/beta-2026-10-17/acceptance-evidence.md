## Evidence provenance and boundary

This record originated on 2026-10-17 from read-only review earlier in that session of the already captured PCV, CHIM, Mind Poisoning, and native-client logs/state, plus explicit operator confirmations. On 2026-10-18, the selected native speech-trace fields were re-read from the retained client log; see the [playback review](playback-review-2026-10-18.md). This is a review summary, not a fresh gameplay run or raw log export. No new game interaction, provider call, server endpoint, test run, database query/write, or installation/removal operation was performed. Raw dialogue and private prompts are deliberately omitted; IDs and fixed outcomes below are retained for bounded correlation.

The tested gameplay evidence names PCV 0.1.20. The [candidate verification receipt](../release-0.1.21/candidate-verification.md) records `package_check` 4/4, five built release assets, and byte-for-byte identity for all 20 non-manifest `server/` payload members across the candidate DWPkg, wrapped TAR, flat discovery TAR, and MO2-embedded DWPkg against the four public 0.1.20 assets; the same current source bytes match the peeled 0.1.20 tag. The only source change was the manifest version and description; other manifest fields and tracked scripts/tests were unchanged. Later [publication](https://github.com/Francisco-boop-001/CHIM-PrivateConversation/blob/98d9e0bae656b83e6d04cbb348ebb4182896b0ff/tasks/release-0.1.21/publication-receipt.md) and [public asset verification](https://github.com/Francisco-boop-001/CHIM-PrivateConversation/blob/98d9e0bae656b83e6d04cbb348ebb4182896b0ff/tasks/release-0.1.21/public-verification.md) receipts record the 0.1.21 BETA release and five matching public assets. ## Release and acceptance limits

- Do not describe any row above as a fresh live test or as testing PCV 0.1.21 itself.
- The candidate verification receipt records the 20/20 non-manifest runtime-payload comparison; the [publication receipt](https://github.com/Francisco-boop-001/CHIM-PrivateConversation/blob/98d9e0bae656b83e6d04cbb348ebb4182896b0ff/tasks/release-0.1.21/publication-receipt.md) and [public asset verification](https://github.com/Francisco-boop-001/CHIM-PrivateConversation/blob/98d9e0bae656b83e6d04cbb348ebb4182896b0ff/tasks/release-0.1.21/public-verification.md) record the published 0.1.21 BETA release. The lead accepted this bounded beta evidence. The record still does not claim a 0.1.21 gameplay run.
- Operator-confirmed repeat, post-END ordinary Standard, and removal outcomes are accepted as confirmations. Their missing timing, IDs, or exact route are limits on detail, not grounds to relabel the confirmed practical outcome as NOT RUN.
- The older helper/API integration fixture used a 0.1.18 validation tree, not the published 0.1.21 payload; 13 runtime files differ and that fixture lacks the later installation guard. It manually assembles scope and calls internal helpers, so it does not verify the public hook, native ACK, or shutdown paths. The retained CHIM core review is limited to source snapshot `cf5030`; resolver/action-executor sources were unavailable, later plugins may be mutable, and the current installed server revision was not verified.
- These results do not certify physical privacy or suppress player/bystander context on SHARMAT scene-start/climax or other Director requests. Those requests remain outside PCV's documented ordinary Standard input scope.
- The evidence is not a complete end-to-end operator checklist, a rollback/backup test, a full MP subject-resolution test, or proof for a different server/client build.
