## Log-access decision

Do not infer the Windows caller from `/proc/net/route` or a private gateway. A WSL kernel marker cannot distinguish a WSL-hosted container or establish the original client behind NAT/port forwarding. The approved policy removes automatic gateway admission and preserves direct loopback requests without forwarding headers plus a nonempty server-authenticated `REMOTE_USER`. Forwarding headers disqualify address-based admission; client-supplied `HTTP_REMOTE_USER` or `PHP_AUTH_USER` is not authentication. This adds no auth system. The restriction covers the diagnostics view and log export, not scene controls. If a Windows/WSL localhost route cannot reach the service and no authenticated route already exists, the server operator must provide authenticated access or inspect logs locally; PCV does not configure authentication or supply a login page.
