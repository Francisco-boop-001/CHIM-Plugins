## Implementation boundary

The bounded reader uses the official ptp_connect() helper to open its own PostgreSQL connection and a read-only repeatable-read transaction. It limits lock waits to 2 seconds and each SQL statement to 3 seconds, while retaining shorter positive limits. It rolls back on failure and closes only its own connection. It preserves the request’s runtime lease and applies the conditional playthrough token guard within the bounded transaction. The read keeps CHIM’s full catalog shape and adds no NPC-count cap. These are per-lock and per-statement bounds, not a whole-request deadline.

Candidate2 was withdrawn because it omitted guards supplied by the core SQL constructor; importing runtime_bootstrap alone does not acquire the restore lease. Candidate3’s restore-guard RED/GREEN fixtures are retained at runtime/restore-guard-red.txt and runtime/restore-guard-green.txt. The final database-backed receipt above is the accepted candidate proof.

Earlier failed attempts remain preserved. Owner-green-1 exposed a fixture that reinserted an existing profile row. Owner-green-2 recorded 16/18 because two valid runtime-barrier refusals emitted newline whitespace while the harness required an exactly empty output. The raw stdout receipts show that whitespace-only output; stderr was empty. The corrected harness accepts whitespace-only stdout while retaining exit-code, timing, and stderr assertions, and owner-green-3 is the final 18/18 result.
